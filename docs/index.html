the heist

your personal art thief

Every day, the heist breaks into the world's great museums and steals a bag of beautiful things for you. A masterpiece from the Met, the Art Institute of Chicago, the Getty, the National Gallery of Art, Yale, the Barnes, Brooklyn, the Walters, Cleveland, Minneapolis, the Smithsonian, the Rijksmuseum, LACMA, the museums of Paris, Birmingham, Hamburg, the national galleries of Oslo, Stockholm and Helsinki, SMK in Copenhagen, and many more. A line lifted from the canon. A find from the Library of Congress vaults. And a place to lay low. On the lam or not.


No takeaways. No insights. No action items. It's a cleanser for an inbox full of pesk, and it asks nothing of you.

Ninety seconds of beauty, then you get on with your day.
